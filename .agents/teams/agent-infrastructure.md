# Agent-infrastructure team (orchestration intent)

> For .agents/ single-source changes (rules, skills, gates, registers,
> generators). Lead = main session.

## Members (constant core)
- governance-steward
- evidence-critic

## Scenario mounts
- test-strategist (gate-impact analysis)

## Product
A governance change set with generator sync clean, full-roster agents check
green, and register transitions recorded.

## Flow
1. Scope: the lead announces the .agents/ surfaces under change.
2. governance-steward reviews single-source discipline, numbering, marker
   regions, register transitions.
3. evidence-critic verifies every claim against the generated faces.
4. Adjudicate; the change lands through `bash accessbase.sh ci`.

## Discipline
- Generated layers are never hand-edited; drift is fixed at the source.
- Every new gate ships with its self-test pair (CONSTRAINTS duty).
