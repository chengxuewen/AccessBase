# Design-system team (orchestration intent)

> For design-token changes and UI-quality bars. Lead = main session.

## Members (constant core)
- web-frontend
- evidence-critic

## Scenario mounts
- test-strategist (visual regression / e2e impact)

## Product
A token/direction decision with before-after evidence and the
ui-ux-review findings table attached.

## Flow
1. ui-ux-review runs the interface-quality method (evidence-backed).
2. web-frontend proposes token/direction changes against the findings.
3. Evidence-critic verifies before/after evidence.
4. Adjudicate; design-system skill remains the implementation authority.

## Discipline
- Reviews do not implement; ui-ux-review findings are inputs.
